### 2026-11-12 | Projects & Tasks Phase 2B: Operations | branch `claude/projects-phase-2b-operations` (draft PR #86; **not merged**); **one additive migration `20270102000100` (not applied by Claude); no hosted SQL**
* **Operations Manager:** a new Projects capability `operations`, per company, given on People & Access through the Projects access (profile **Operations Manager**) by a founder or a Projects access manager. It opens Operations, implies PM assignment for handovers and for reassigning a live project's Project Manager (not for creating an internal project or changing Sales links), reads that company's projects in full without joining them, and is an explicit path into the company's project chats. Its holder can comment on a task, raise a correction and post in the project's conversation; it cannot add a file or a link, create, assign, move or review tasks, edit the Delivery Plan, decide corrections or changes, manage or complete a project, or see restricted files or the commercial record. No Sales, Finance, HR or Warehouse right. Nobody holds it until it is given.
* **Navigation:** a top-level **Operations** with **Overview** (`/operations`) and **Upcoming Work** (`/operations/upcoming`), before Projects & Tasks; in the More drawer on a phone.
* **Overview:** Needs my decision (Project Manager not assigned, handover stalled after 3 days, blocker older than 2 days or waiting on someone else, installation at risk from 14 days before its start until the event ends, Review equipment booking, unused reservations not released), Coming up from Sales, Upcoming delivery with the most relevant issue, Team and warehouse pressure. Recorded facts only; every item links to the screen where its owner acts.
* **Upcoming Work and the enquiry's Operations planning panel:** Sales (owner or founder) flags an enquiry and gives an expected decision date, a dismantling date, whether dates are tentative, expected teams, what is still to confirm and a note. Operations reads it through an allowlist (no value, quote, brief or note), names a provisional Project Manager, a risk, a capacity conflict or a question; nothing is booked. Sales answers on the enquiry. Sales gets no notice of an Operations update.
* **Event edition:** copied from the enquiry to the handover when it is saved or submitted and to the project when it is accepted; shown on the Work details card, the directory (line and **Event edition** filter), the handover brief, the Sales event page (**Delivery projects**) and in the Closeout Report evidence. Existing projects and accepted handovers stay unlinked.

Inventory Today's readiness sentence counts lines to buy or rent apart.
* **Chat:** Operations Managers in the company's project groups (tag **Operations**, a note in the thread, "(Operations)" in the mention list); groups of archived projects under a collapsed **Archived (N)**.
