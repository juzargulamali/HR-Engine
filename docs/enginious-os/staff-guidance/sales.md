## How Sales & CRM works
- id: sales-overview
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: sales crm; sales and crm; how does sales work; how sales works; sales module; sales process; sales overview; explain sales; what is sales; sales workflow; enquiry process; enquiry to handover; how does the crm work
- links: Enquiries -> /sales/opportunities; Follow-ups -> /sales/follow-ups; Clients & Agencies -> /sales/accounts; Reports -> /sales/reports

Sales & CRM follows each enquiry from the first message to the handover. An enquiry moves through the stages New enquiry, Qualified, Proposal preparation, Proposal sent, Quote sent and Negotiation, and ends as Won or Lost. Every open enquiry keeps one next action, so Follow-ups always shows what to do next. A won enquiry is handed over to the project team; winning it does not by itself mean the project was delivered.

1. Add the enquiry in Enquiries (title, source, agency or end client, and the next action with its date).
2. Work it from Follow-ups: log each call, email or meeting on the enquiry and plan the next step.
3. Move it to the next stage as it progresses. Proposal sent needs a proposal recorded as sent; Quote sent needs the Zoho Books quote reference.
4. Build the estimate from the enquiry when a price is needed. Prices stay in the Estimate screen.
5. Mark it Won or Lost from the enquiry page, then complete the handover for a won enquiry. Reports shows the pipeline and results for your company.

## See your overdue follow-ups
- id: sales-overdue-follow-ups
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: overdue follow-ups; overdue follow ups; overdue followups; my overdue; late follow-ups; what is overdue; overdue actions
- links: Follow-ups -> /sales/follow-ups

A follow-up is overdue once its due time has passed and it is still open. The Follow-ups page groups them at the top. In JARVIS you can also type "Show my overdue Sales follow-ups" to list your own for the selected company.

1. Open Follow-ups in Sales. Overdue items are listed first.
2. Open the enquiry from the item, do the work, then complete or reschedule the follow-up.

## Add an enquiry
- id: sales-add-enquiry
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: add enquiry; new enquiry; create enquiry; add an enquiry; create an enquiry; log an enquiry; new inquiry; add inquiry; create inquiry; quick add; enter a lead; new lead
- links: Enquiries -> /sales/opportunities; Full new-enquiry form -> /sales/new

Enquiries live in Sales. The quick form needs a title, where it came from, an agency or an end client (or both) and the next action with its date.

1. Open Enquiries in Sales.
2. Press Add enquiry.
3. Fill in the title, the source, the agency and/or end client, and what happens next and when.
4. Save. The enquiry opens so you can add more details.

## See and plan follow-ups
- id: sales-follow-ups
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: follow-up; follow up; followup; next action; to-do; todo; reminder; overdue; due today; schedule next action; add follow-up; add a follow-up; log a follow-up
- links: Follow-ups -> /sales/follow-ups; Enquiries -> /sales/opportunities

The Follow-ups page lists what you need to do next on your enquiries. Every enquiry that is still being worked needs one open next action, so planning the next step is part of finishing the current one.

1. Open Follow-ups in Sales to see what is due.
2. To add one, open the enquiry and use Add follow-up (or Schedule next action in the side panel).
3. Choose what to do, the date and who it is for.

## Log a call, email or meeting on an enquiry
- id: sales-log-update
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: log a call; log call; log an email; log a meeting; add update; add an update; activity; record a call; note on an enquiry; meeting notes; add a note; timeline; log activity
- links: Enquiries -> /sales/opportunities

Updates are the enquiry's diary. Add one after a call, an email or a meeting; you can plan the next step in the same box.

1. Open the enquiry.
2. Press Add update.
3. Choose the type (call, email, meeting, note or client response) and write what happened.
4. Turn on Plan the next step if you want a follow-up created at the same time.

## Edit an enquiry's details
- id: sales-edit-details
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: edit enquiry; edit an enquiry; change enquiry; edit details; change the title; change the location; change the dates; update enquiry; amend enquiry; change the event date
- links: Enquiries -> /sales/opportunities

The enquiry's owner, or a Sales founder, can edit its details while it is open. Once it is Won only a founder can change the value, and a Lost enquiry cannot be edited.

1. Open the enquiry.
2. Press Edit details.
3. Change what you need and save. If someone else changed it first you will be asked to reload.

## Change an enquiry's stage
- id: sales-move-stage
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: change stage; move stage; change status; move to; stage; status of an enquiry; pipeline; board; qualified; negotiation; proposal sent; quote sent; on hold; put on hold; resume
- links: Enquiries -> /sales/opportunities; Board view -> /sales/opportunities?view=board

The enquiry's owner or a Sales founder moves it: one step back, or any step forward. Proposal sent needs a proposal recorded as sent first, and Quote sent needs the Zoho Books quote reference. Won, Lost and On hold have their own forms.

1. Open the enquiry (or use the Board view of Enquiries).
2. Choose Move to another stage and pick the stage.
3. If the stage needs something first, the screen says what.

## Mark an enquiry won or lost, and hand it over
- id: sales-close-handover
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: mark won; mark as won; won; lost; mark lost; close an enquiry; close enquiry; handover; hand over; hand-over; reopen; re-open
- links: Enquiries -> /sales/opportunities

Won and Lost are closed on their own form, which asks for the evidence or the reason. After Won, the handover to Projects is prepared from the enquiry. JARVIS does not close, reopen or hand over enquiries: do those on the enquiry page.

1. Open the enquiry.
2. Choose the Won or Lost action and fill in the form.
3. After Won, open the handover from the enquiry.

## Find or add a client or agency
- id: sales-clients-agencies
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: add a client; add client; new client; add an agency; add agency; new agency; register; client or agency; clients and agencies; organisation; organization; account; duplicate client; import clients; excel import
- links: Clients & Agencies -> /sales/accounts; Import from Excel -> /sales/accounts/import

Clients & Agencies holds every organisation. Search first so you do not add the same one twice. An organisation can be an agency, a direct client, an end client or a technology company, and an enquiry's agency and end-client boxes offer only organisations with the matching role.

1. Open Clients & Agencies and search by name.
2. If it is not there, add it with its roles (and country and website if you know them).
3. To add many at once, use Import from Excel.

## Build an estimate
- id: sales-estimates
- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: estimate; estimates; quotation; quote; boq; price an enquiry; pricing; vat; revision; pdf of the estimate; excel of the estimate
- links: Enquiries -> /sales/opportunities

Estimates are built on the enquiry's Estimates tab in three steps: details, items, then preview and finalise. An estimate is budgetary: it cannot be recorded as sent. Official quotations go through the Zoho Books workflow.

1. Open the enquiry and go to the Estimates tab.
2. Create an estimate and fill in the details and the items.
3. Preview, then finalise. A finalised estimate is frozen; make a revision to change it.

## Sales settings

- area: sales
- status: published
- keywords: sales settings; reference code; response target; working calendar; duplicate window; approval policy; vat choices; settings

Each company's Sales settings (reference code, currency, response target, working calendar, duplicate window, owner e-mail, approval policy and the estimate VAT choices) are kept on one page, for people who manage Sales settings or are founders.
