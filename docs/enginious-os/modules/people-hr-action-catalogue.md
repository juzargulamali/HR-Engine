# People & HR: action catalogue (employee directory, profile and onboarding)

**Status.** Written for **People & HR Phase 1** on branch `feat/people-hr-phase1`: a draft pull request, **not merged**, hosted migration `20270115000100` **applied by the owner (owner-confirmed, not seen by Claude)**, Production not deployed from this branch. Required CI: first run recorded on the pull request (not part of this file). Fifteen rows in all. Every action on the employee directory and profile that changes data: where it is, who may do it and where that is enforced, what it changes, how a retry behaves and when to stop. Actions of other People & HR pages (leave requests, attendance, letters, payroll runs, assets, reimbursements) are not in this phase's scope and keep their rows in [`../03-roles-access-and-approvals.md`](../03-roles-access-and-approvals.md).

**A documented action is not an approved JARVIS tool.** JARVIS cannot read or change People & HR (see [`jarvis.md`](./jarvis.md) section 18 and [`jarvis-rulebook.md`](./jarvis-rulebook.md) section 10); nothing here grants it anything.

Sources: `P1` = `supabase/migrations/20270115000100_people_hr_phase1.sql`. Server actions are in `web/lib/actions/` (`employeeOnboarding.ts`, `employeeSetup.ts`, `employmentChanges.ts`, `employeeDocuments.ts`, `employees.ts`, `peopleAccess.ts`, `performance.ts`); permission helpers in `packages/domain/src/permissions/employees.ts`. "Unchanged" means the action existed before Phase 1 and its code and rule were not edited; it was not re-read line by line for this catalogue, so its enforcement column names the policy that decides, as the earlier Map did (`Unverified` where marked).

## 3. What no action here does

* No action here sends an invitation, resets a password, or changes a role or module access: those are People & Access ([`people-access-invites-and-password-links.md`](./people-access-invites-and-password-links.md)).
* No action cancels a scheduled change (an open decision in [`people-hr.md`](./people-hr.md) section 11).
* No action reads or writes another company's employee: the company is taken from the employee row on the server, and the database policies refuse the rest (`RLS`: an HR Admin of one company is refused identically for another company's employee, pay and documents).
