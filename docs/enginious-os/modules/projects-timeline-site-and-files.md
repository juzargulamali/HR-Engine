## 2. Timeline tab: the Plan section shell

**How to reach it.** Open a project, then the tab **Timeline** (second tab; `projectTabs`, `apps/web/src/lib/projects/workspace-nav.ts`). Address `/projects/<id>/plan`. **Address parameters** (`parsePlanParams`, `planHref`, `apps/web/src/lib/projects/plan-view.ts`; `parseCalendarParams`, `components/projects/calendar/helpers.ts`)

|---|---|---|

## 3. Delivery timeline and Site production timeline (List and Timeline modes)

**How to reach it.** Timeline tab, "Which plan" = Delivery (`/projects/<id>/plan` or `?plan=delivery`) or Site (`?plan=site`), "How to show it" = List (default) or Timeline (`mode=timeline`).

**Who can see it.** Members and founders (section 2). Changing anything: Manager only, and only while the project is not completed or archived (`canEditPlan = view.canManage && !finished`, `plan/page.tsx`; `proj_require_manager` and `proj_require_open_project` in each function).

**Purpose.** An editable list of stages for the plan. Panel title "Delivery timeline" with the description "The creative and technical build, from moodboard to full test." or "Site production timeline" with "What happens on site, from access to dismantling." (`PLAN_LABELS`, `packages/domain/src/projectTemplates.ts`; `PlanSection`, `plan/_sections.tsx`).

**What the list shows.** Columns Item, When, Status, and an actions column for a Manager. Item: the title, then "Offered on the client timeline" or "Internal only", then the linked milestone as "milestone “<title>”", then the internal notes. When: a plain date range for a date-only item; for a timed item the site's own time first and, when the item's zone is not Dubai, a second line ending "(your time)" which is always Dubai time on this screen (`VIEW_TZ = "Asia/Dubai"` in `plan/_sections.tsx`; `planItemWhen`, `timeline-helpers.ts`; `describeTimedEvent`, `packages/domain/src/projectCalendar.ts`); "Not scheduled yet" for an undated item. Status: Planned, In progress, Done, Cancelled. Order: dated items first, earliest first, undated last, then the saved order, then the title (`sortPlanItems`). Cancelled items stay in the list.

## 5. Site bookings (Site plan)

**How to reach it.** Timeline tab, Site, List or Timeline mode: `/projects/<id>/plan?plan=site`, panel "Site bookings" (anchor `#bookings`). A conflict check has its own address: `/projects/<id>/plan?plan=site&mode=list&check=<bookingId>#bookings`. In-app notices about a booking link to the legacy address `/projects/<id>/timeline`, which opens the Delivery list, not the Site plan (`apps/web/src/lib/projects/notification-text.ts`; section 8).

**Who can see it.** Members and founders read every booking of the project (SELECT policy on `proj_site_bookings`). Who may add, edit, confirm or cancel a booking of a person: the Booker of that person (section 1). The page offers **Add booking** when the project is not finished and the person can book at least one of the project's people (`canBookAny`), and offers the row controls when the person can book the booked person (`mayBook`; a Manager for a booked person no longer in the people list) (`plan/page.tsx`). The database repeats it: "Only the Project Manager, or the head of that person's department team, can book them on site" (`proj_save_booking`), "... can confirm this booking" (`proj_confirm_booking`), "... can cancel this booking" (`proj_cancel_booking`). A plain member books nobody (`timeline-helpers.test.ts`: "lets a manager book everybody, a team head only their own team, and a plain member nobody").

**Purpose.** Reserve one person at a physical site, in the site's own time, with preparation and travel allowances, and find double bookings across every project. **What goes into a version** (`proj_build_plan_payload`, `20261120000500_...sql`): the project's reference, name, client name, location and site time zone; client-facing milestones that are not cancelled (title, due date); client-facing Delivery items that are not cancelled (title, dates); client-facing Site items that are not cancelled (title, dates or local times with zone); client-facing dependencies (label, kind, expected date and time, organisation, confirmation). Never: notes, contacts, bookings, tasks, files.

**The "issued client timeline is out of date" rule** (`proj_plan_outdated`, same file). The function builds the payload above from the live data and compares its hash with the hash stored on the latest version that has not been replaced. It answers true when they differ, false when they match, and nothing when no version has been issued or the person cannot see the project. So the latest version is marked outdated after any change to something in the payload: a client-facing item added, removed, cancelled, renamed or re-dated, an item ticked or unticked, a client-facing dependency or its confirmation changed, and also a change to the project's name, reference, client name, location or site time zone. Putting everything back makes it current again. Internal-only changes never mark it. It is shown here (badge and warning) and, to a Manager of a client project, on the Work page: "The issued client timeline is out of date: the client-facing plan has changed since it was issued. Issue a new version from Files & client plan." (`[id]/work/page.tsx`). Nobody is notified.
