## 1. Who sees what

* Access is a company-scoped Sales grant (`crm_company_grants`), never an HR role: `getCrmContext` (`apps/web/src/lib/crm/context.ts`), the shell `apps/web/src/app/(app)/sales/layout.tsx`. No grant: an access notice (no data). Grants unreadable: "Sales & CRM could not load" (`ctx.loadFailed`), never "no access".
* A person with any grant for a company reads every enquiry of that company, whoever owns it (`crm_has_access(company_id)` policies in `20261115000100_crm_core_tables.sql`; confirmed in `sales-clients-and-agencies.md` section 5). The owner or a Sales founder changes it (`crm_require_owner_or_founder`, `20261115000200_crm_workflow_functions.sql`; UI mirror `canEditOpportunity`, `packages/domain/src/permissions/crm.ts`).
* Capabilities (`CRM_CAPABILITIES`, same file): `founder`, `assign_owner`, `confirm_availability`, `manage_settings`, `manage_approval_rules`, `manage_access`, `view_audit`, `reopen_closed`. `founder` implies every other one (`hasCrmCapability`).
* Optimistic concurrency: every enquiry write that carries `expectedVersion` is first compared by the app (`staleMessage`, `apps/web/src/lib/actions/crm.ts`: "This enquiry was changed by someone else. Reload the page ...") and again by the database (`crm_check_version`, errcode `40001`).

### 3.4 Role-aware organisation pickers (one search-as-you-type field each)

Agency, End client and Event / Exhibition are each **one field** (`SearchCombobox`, `components/sales/combobox.tsx`): type, see the matches directly below the input, choose by mouse, touch or keyboard (arrows, Enter, Escape); built to the ARIA combobox pattern. Matching: every word typed must appear in the name or the detail, names starting with it first, at most 8 shown. Under each match: for an account its **country (or place) and website**, to tell similar names apart; for an edition its venue and start date. The shared directory is searched (every company's accounts). **Typing alone never selects or creates anything**: editing the text after a choice clears the choice until a match is chosen again, and Enter picks only a highlighted match. Tests: `combobox.test.tsx`, `inquiry-organisation-pickers.test.tsx`.

**Create event or edition (New enquiry, Quick add and, since 2026-11-11, Edit details: see 4.1).** Under the Event / Exhibition field, and named in the "Nothing matches" line, is **Create event or edition** (`EventEditionPicker` `onCreate`, `app/(app)/sales/new/new-enquiry-form.tsx`). It opens the shared event and edition form ([sales-events-and-exhibitions.md](./sales-events-and-exhibitions.md) section 3.5) **prefilled with the text typed in the field** (only while nothing is chosen) **and the enquiry's Event start and Event end**, so a new event with its first edition, or a missing edition of an existing event, can be added without leaving the enquiry. **It is its own explicit save and saves only the event and edition**; the enquiry is saved only by its own button (`createOpportunity`, unchanged, then `crm_set_opportunity_edition` under the unchanged rule: the enquiry's owner or a founder). On success the dialog closes, **every field of the enquiry is as it was and the new edition is selected** (`useCreatedEditions` adds it to the field's list at once). Cancel, or a refused save (a duplicate name, bad dates), leaves the draft untouched and nothing linked; a refused save stays open with what was typed. Who: any Sales grant, the same right as Add event (`crm_add_event_with_edition`, `crm_save_edition`). When the events could not be loaded the field says so and offers neither the list nor the action. On Edit details the field keeps its link to Events & Exhibitions. ## 4. Enquiry page (`/sales/opportunities/[id]`, `page.tsx`)

The page reads the enquiry through `loadOpportunityDetailResult` (`lib/crm/data.ts`) and tells **four outcomes apart**: a malformed id or an enquiry the person cannot see is the not-found page (`notFound`; the database does not distinguish missing from invisible); a refused read (permission error) says "You do not have access to this enquiry"; a failed read says "Could not load this enquiry ... This is not a missing enquiry" with Try again and **shows no tabs and no action**; a successful read shows the page. The names of the parties are part of the header, so their failure is the enquiry's. **Any other section can fail on its own** (contacts, activities, follow-ups, history, files, proposals, Zoho references, approvals, handover, closures): that section says "Could not load ..." (never "No open follow-up", "No proposal recorded yet", "No files or links yet", "Nothing has been recorded yet" or "None"), and the actions that depend on it are not offered: Follow-ups (add, complete, reschedule, cancel; also the "No next action is scheduled" warning), contacts management, the log-an-activity form when the follow-ups failed, proposal versions and their buttons (versions, files and approvals together), files (add, remove), the Zoho form, approval request and decision, the estimates list, **Move stage** when the proposals or the Zoho references failed (its requirement hints would be wrong), and **Reopen** when the handover record failed.
