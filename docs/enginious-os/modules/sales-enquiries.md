## 1. Who sees what

* Access is a company-scoped Sales grant (`crm_company_grants`), never an HR role: `getCrmContext` (`apps/web/src/lib/crm/context.ts`), the shell `apps/web/src/app/(app)/sales/layout.tsx`. No grant: an access notice (no data). Grants unreadable: "Sales & CRM could not load" (`ctx.loadFailed`), never "no access".
* A person with any grant for a company reads every enquiry of that company, whoever owns it (`crm_has_access(company_id)` policies in `20261115000100_crm_core_tables.sql`; confirmed in `sales-clients-and-agencies.md` section 5). The owner or a Sales founder changes it (`crm_require_owner_or_founder`, `20261115000200_crm_workflow_functions.sql`; UI mirror `canEditOpportunity`, `packages/domain/src/permissions/crm.ts`).
* Capabilities (`CRM_CAPABILITIES`, same file): `founder`, `assign_owner`, `confirm_availability`, `manage_settings`, `manage_approval_rules`, `manage_access`, `view_audit`, `reopen_closed`. `founder` implies every other one (`hasCrmCapability`).
* Optimistic concurrency: every enquiry write that carries `expectedVersion` is first compared by the app (`staleMessage`, `apps/web/src/lib/actions/crm.ts`: "This enquiry was changed by someone else. Reload the page ...") and again by the database (`crm_check_version`, errcode `40001`).

### 3.4 Role-aware organisation pickers (one search-as-you-type field each)

Agency, End client and Event / Exhibition are each **one field** (`SearchCombobox`, `components/sales/combobox.tsx`): type, see the matches directly below the input, choose by mouse, touch or keyboard (arrows, Enter, Escape); built to the ARIA combobox pattern. Matching: every word typed must appear in the name or the detail, names starting with it first, at most 8 shown. Under each match: for an account its **country (or place) and website**, to tell similar names apart; for an edition its venue and start date. The shared directory is searched (every company's accounts). **Typing alone never selects or creates anything**: editing the text after a choice clears the choice until a match is chosen again, and Enter picks only a highlighted match. Tests: `combobox.test.tsx`, `inquiry-organisation-pickers.test.tsx`.

**Create event or edition (New enquiry, Quick add and, since 2026-11-11, Edit details: see 4.1).** Under the Event / Exhibition field, and named in the "Nothing matches" line, is **Create event or edition** (`EventEditionPicker` `onCreate`, `app/(app)/sales/new/new-enquiry-form.tsx`). It opens the shared event and edition form ([sales-events-and-exhibitions.md](./sales-events-and-exhibitions.md) section 3.5) **prefilled with the text typed in the field** (only while nothing is chosen) **and the enquiry's Event start and Event end**, so a new event with its first edition, or a missing edition of an existing event, can be added without leaving the enquiry. **It is its own explicit save and saves only the event and edition**; the enquiry is saved only by its own button (`createOpportunity`, unchanged, then `crm_set_opportunity_edition` under the unchanged rule: the enquiry's owner or a founder). On success the dialog closes, **every field of the enquiry is as it was and the new edition is selected** (`useCreatedEditions` adds it to the field's list at once). Cancel, or a refused save (a duplicate name, bad dates), leaves the draft untouched and nothing linked; a refused save stays open with what was typed. Who: any Sales grant, the same right as Add event (`crm_add_event_with_edition`, `crm_save_edition`). When the events could not be loaded the field says so and offers neither the list nor the action. On Edit details the field keeps its link to Events & Exhibitions. Tests: `components/sales/event-edition-dialog.test.tsx` ("Create event or edition from Quick Add": prefill, draft kept, new edition selected, enquiry not saved; refused save and Cancel). **Not verified:** the full New enquiry page in a component test (it shares the picker, the hook and the dialog; only Quick add is mounted in the test) and both forms signed-in.


The Agency picker offers organisations with the agency role; the End client picker offers end-client or direct-client roles; an organisation with no roles recorded reads as its legacy `kind`; one organisation can appear in both (`apps/web/src/lib/crm/organisation-roles.ts`, `offeredFor`). ## 4. Enquiry page (`/sales/opportunities/[id]`, `page.tsx`)

The page reads the enquiry through `loadOpportunityDetailResult` (`lib/crm/data.ts`) and tells **four outcomes apart**: a malformed id or an enquiry the person cannot see is the not-found page (`notFound`; the database does not distinguish missing from invisible); a refused read (permission error) says "You do not have access to this enquiry"; a failed read says "Could not load this enquiry ... This is not a missing enquiry" with Try again and **shows no tabs and no action**; a successful read shows the page. ### 9.5 Overview, Scope & dates, Activity & history

* **Overview** (`overview-panel.tsx`): in the order the work is done. Left: Follow-ups (with **Log activity** and **Add follow-up**), Who is involved (with the contacts), Brief, Operations planning. Right: Event and key dates (with Source, Created, First response and, when there is one this person may see, **Came from outreach**), Commercial status, **Latest activity** (the three newest timeline entries, with "Open the full history"; `latest-activity.tsx`). Stage, owner, value and the main action are not repeated here.
* **Came from outreach** (`outreach-origin.tsx`, `lib/crm/enquiry-outreach.ts`): the outreach linked to the enquiry (`crm_outreach.opportunity_id`), up to three, each a link to the account, plus a link to the Outreach list. Nothing is shown when there is none this person may see; a failed read says "Could not load whether this enquiry came from outreach."
* **Scope & dates** (`scope-panel.tsx`): Edit details is at the top of the tab with the sentence that equipment availability was checked against exactly these dates, technologies and quantities. When the check is stale a warning says "Availability must be checked again." with a link to Commercial.
* **Activity & history** (`activity-panel.tsx`, `add-activity-form.tsx`, `timeline.ts`, `timeline-list.tsx`): the form is headed **Log activity** (fields "What kind", "What happened (the outcome)", "This was one of the planned follow-ups", "Plan the next step"; button "Add to timeline" or "Complete and add to timeline"). The timeline filter is **All**, **People's updates**, **System** (was All activity / Comments and notes). A completed follow-up is ONE entry: the follow-up row, the internal note the database writes ("Follow-up done: <title>. <outcome>") and the person's own update with the same words are folded together for display when they are the same person, the same words and the same moment (5 seconds for the note, 2 minutes for the update). **Display only: no row is deleted or changed**; anything else stays its own entry. A long entry (over 280 characters or four lines) starts folded with **Show more**. An entry the app recorded is marked "recorded by the app".
* **Completing a follow-up from Log activity** (`addActivity`, `lib/actions/crm.ts`): the follow-up is completed with the words as its outcome, and the person's own update is **always written as its own row, whatever its kind (a Note included)**. That row carries the **Internal note** choice exactly as ticked or left unticked, the kind of contact, and (for the first call, email or meeting) the first-response time. The completion also writes its own internal note with the same words; both records are kept and the timeline shows the completion **once**, with the internal mark of the person's row (`timeline.ts` `matchCompletions`; tests `timeline.test.ts` and `lib/actions/crm.test.ts`, both the ticked and the unticked case). An earlier state of this branch skipped the second Note; the owner asked for the choice to be preserved, so the skip was removed before merge.
