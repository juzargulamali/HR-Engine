## 4. Definition of the counts

### 4.1 Directory columns and the relationship badge

The directory counts through the SAME function as the account page: `linkedInquiries` (`apps/web/src/lib/crm/clients-agencies/account.ts`) is used by `deriveAccount` (account page and export Summary) and by `enquiryStats` (directory). Tests: `relationship.test.ts`, `account.test.ts`.

* **Enquiries**: this company's enquiries that name the account as agency OR end client, once each. An enquiry naming the same account in both roles counts once; another company's enquiries are never counted. "Enquiries" means CRM enquiries (opportunities), NOT quotes Enginious sent.
* **Won enquiries**: linked enquiries in stage Won (`isWonStage`). A project in progress or a completed project is NOT a win and is not looked at.
* **Open enquiry**: a linked enquiry that is not Won and not Lost (`isOpenStage`; On hold counts as open), the same rule as "Open" on the account page.
* **Last enquiry**: the Dubai calendar day the newest linked enquiry was recorded (`crm_opportunities.created_at`, shown in Dubai time). No linked enquiry: "None".
* **Relationship badge**, first match wins (`relationshipOf`, `relationship.ts`): (1) **No enquiries recorded** (grey): no linked enquiry; (2) **Dormant** (purple): the last enquiry is 90 or more Dubai calendar days before today AND there is no open enquiry (89 days is not dormant, 90 is; an open enquiry older than 90 days prevents Dormant; Dormant beats Won work, so an old win with nothing open reads Dormant); (3) **Won work** (green): at least one Won enquiry; (4) **No wins yet** (amber). The 90 days are `DORMANT_DAYS`; "today" and each date are Dubai days like the rest of CRM (`dubaiDate`).
* **Could not load**: when the enquiries cannot be read the figures and the badge are null and shown as "Could not load", never 0 and never a badge.

### 4.2 Bulk selection scope

* The select-all box (table header) and the "Select all N on this page" button select exactly the rows shown (at most the first 100 of the filtered result). There is no "select all matching" beyond the page (decision: page only, so nothing can be selected out of sight).
* A persistent line reads "N selected of M shown (Assign outreach applies to the N selected)".
* The selection is cleared whenever the company, search, any filter, the relationship chip or the sort changes (the page keys the selection by those values), and after a successful assignment. Nothing selected is ever kept out of view.
* The Assign outreach dialog lists every selected account and its count, and submits only those ids; the action and the database check the right again as before.

* **Inquiries**: every inquiry of the selected company that names this account as agency or end client, counted once even if the account is both. Another company's inquiries are never counted.
* **Direct** / **Through an agency** / **As the agency**: three exclusive buckets that add up to Inquiries. Direct: this account is the end client and no other agency is recorded (or the same organisation is on both sides). Through an agency: this account is the end client and a different agency is recorded (the page names it). As the agency: this account is the agency (the page names the end client, if recorded).
* **Won inquiries**: stage Won. A won inquiry is not a completed project.
* **Completed projects**: Projects (`proj_projects.crm_opportunity_id`) of those inquiries with status Completed (or a completion date), counted once per project, read through the viewer's own session. A person who cannot open a project (Projects access or a Sales link is needed; completed projects are visible to founders, members and Sales-linked people only, `proj_can_view_summary`) does not get it counted: where the handover exists (`proj_handover_for_opportunity`, readable by any Sales grantee) but the project cannot be opened, the page says "Project exists; status not visible to you" and how many were left out.
* **Avoiding double counting across views**: an inquiry shows on the agency's page and again on the end client's page, because each shows that account's own work. Each page counts it once and says so; counts of two accounts must not be added together.
* **Awaiting reply**: only for a quote recorded as sent (a proposal with status `sent` and its `sent_on`, or a Zoho `quote_sent_on`), on an inquiry that is still open (not Won, Lost or On hold), with NO `client_response` activity dated on or after the sent date. Otherwise the plain status is shown (Draft, Sent, Superseded, Accepted, Not sent; a recorded reply reads "Sent · reply recorded <date>"). A call or email of ours is not a reply. An inquiry with two awaiting quotes counts once.
* **Follow-ups due**: open follow-ups of those inquiries; overdue and due-today are by Dubai date.

## 5. What each assignment confers (verified in the SQL and by RLS tests)

| Inquiry owner | nothing extra | edits, moves and closes own inquiry; may assign its follow-ups to others; edits contacts linked to its open inquiries; (new) edits an organisation it is on | `crm_require_owner_or_founder`, `crm_create_follow_up`, `crm_can_edit_contact`, `crm_can_edit_organisation` |

Nothing was widened. Reassigning a follow-up never changes the account owner or the inquiry owner, and assigning an account owner never changes a follow-up or an inquiry owner (RLS and action tests). The older inquiry-owner reassignment `crm_assign_owner` still moves the previous owner's open follow-ups to the new owner; that existing behaviour is untouched and is about the INQUIRY, not the account.

## 6. Open points (decisions for the owner; the safe behaviour is built)

1. **Shared organisation records.** Organisations and contacts are shared reference data readable by any Sales grantee of any company (existing, `crm_organisations_select`). The directory therefore lists every shared organisation; everything company-specific (owner, lead source, exhibition, inquiries, quotes, follow-ups, projects, activity) is strictly the selected company's. Making the organisation list itself per company is a change to the existing sharing model and was not made.
2. **Account owner confers no edit right.** An imported or unassigned profile can be edited by its creator, a Sales founder, or an inquiry owner who has it on an open inquiry. Letting the account owner edit it would be a new permission and was not added.
3. **Account-level follow-ups.** Resolved by the corrections (section 9.3). Remaining limit: an account follow-up has no inquiry, so it produces no in-app reminder or e-mail (`crm_generate_reminders` joins to an inquiry); it shows on Follow-ups, My Day, reports and the account page.
4. **Owner by e-mail (import).** `crm_people()` returns e-mail addresses only to a Sales founder, so e-mail-to-owner resolution works only for a founder; for anyone else `resolveOwnerByEmail` answers "emails_hidden".

### 8.2 Rules the import follows

* **Company boundary.** Rows land only in the company chosen on the page, and only if the person holds a Sales grant for it (checked in the route before any read; the database re-checks every write). The shared record gets the file's name, type, country, website and notes; owner, lead source and exhibition are this company's profile only.
* **Permission.** Creating needs any CRM grant (`crm_create_organisation_profile`); changing an existing organisation needs the existing edit rule (`crm_can_edit_organisation`: its creator, a Sales founder, or the owner of an open inquiry it is on); setting an owner needs `assign_owner`. The import adds no right: where the database refuses, that organisation is reported and nothing is forced.
* **Owner e-mail.** Resolved with `resolveOwnerByEmail` against the people the signed-in person can see. `crm_people()` shows e-mail addresses only to a Sales founder, so anyone else gets "owner could not be verified" and the account stays Unassigned. Unknown, not in this company, ambiguous and "you cannot assign owners" are separate messages. No user account is ever created and no function other than the five above is called.
* **Existing workbook.** Country (`location`) is profile data and never the owner. Types: Agency, Direct client, End client, Technology Company map to the four roles.
* **Safe reruns.** Enforced in the database (section 9.1), not in the server process: locks, fill-only writes, a batch key and the preview fingerprint. The earlier per-process lock was removed.

### 8.3 First-import expectation (counts only)

The real workbook was parsed once, read-only, outside the repository, printing counts only: 65 source rows, 1 unnamed row skipped, 6 repeated rows collapsed, 58 candidates = 48 agencies + 4 direct clients + 6 technology companies, 0 end clients, 0 unknown types, 0 unrecognised countries, 0 row errors. This equals the expectation. The CI fixture `legacyWorkbook()` (`import/test-fixtures.ts`) is fictional and generated in memory with the same headers and counts. Matching against the CRM was not run (nothing was connected to any hosted data).

### 8.4 Limits and open points

1. **Double confirm across servers.** Closed by section 9.1: the database function locks per normalised name and website domain and keeps a batch key, so overlapping confirms from any people, files or servers cannot create duplicates or overwrite.
2. **Tested against Postgres.** The import's database behaviour is proved by `packages/rls-tests/test/crm-import-safety.rls.test.ts` with separate sessions; `import.test.ts` proves what the application sends. A local-stack browser run exists (`apps/web/os-shell-checks/clients-agencies-flow.mjs`, section 9.6).
3. **4 MB, not 5 MB**, so the platform's 4.5 MB request cap never cuts the upload before our message.
4. **Migration.** Part 2 added none; the corrections of section 9 changed `20261219000100` in place (not applied on any hosted project).
5. **Dependency.** `exceljs` 4.4.0 (apps/web only); last release December 2024, widely used, no maintained alternative that writes and reads `.xlsx` with styles was chosen over it. The unmaintained `xlsx` package was avoided.
6. **Contacts of an existing organisation** are only added, never updated (a new title or phone for a known person is not applied). **Filling an owner** applies only to an unassigned account.
7. **Large lists** in the preview are shortened to 150 per group (every row is still checked and counted).

## 10. Pre-inquiry outreach (assigned sales worklist, no inquiry needed)

An assigned worklist for approaching NEW prospects and re-engaging EXISTING accounts before any inquiry exists. It is not a reminder and not a deal: no inquiry or placeholder deal is ever created. Source: migration `20261219000100_crm_clients_agencies.sql` section 8 (corrected in place; still applied on no hosted project; one migration, one owner pack, see `docs/clients-agencies-deployment.md`).

**What was reused and what was built.** Reused: the `assign_owner` capability (founder implies it) as the only right to assign, `crm_user_has_company_access` for the eligible-person rule and `eligibleStaff` for the list of people, `crm_has_access` for company scoping, the audit trigger, `LoadError`, `Modal` / `ActionForm` / `Field`, the existing `crm_create_opportunity` flow and its role-aware pickers for "Create inquiry". NOT reused: `crm_activities` (every row requires an inquiry and cascades with it, and its readers count rows against inquiries) and `crm_follow_ups` (one reminder date, no status, no history); outreach has its own tables so no existing reader or screen changes.

**Definitions (fixed lists, database-checked).**

History (`crm_outreach_events`) is append-only: a guard trigger refuses update and delete for everyone, and there is no DML policy. **Reassignment and the history (rule, verified by tests; the read rule itself was already right and needed no change).** Events are never filtered by who logged them: they follow the outreach row's visibility only. After a reassignment (1) the NEW assignee reads the FULL history, including every earlier event logged by the former assignee, by leads and by the founder, through the account's Outreach section and My outreach; (2) the FORMER assignee can no longer read the outreach row, its events or the history, My outreach no longer lists it, and the functions refuse them ("Only the person this outreach is assigned to..."), unless they are separately authorised (for example they hold `assign_owner`, in which case they still read it all); (3) the history is append-only through the move: every earlier event is unchanged and in order, and the reassignment itself is added as one `reassigned` event naming the former assignee, the new assignee and who moved it.
