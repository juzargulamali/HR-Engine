### 2.2 Estimate page: three steps (`.../quotations/[proposalId]/page.tsx`, `editor.tsx`, `lib/crm/quotations/builder-state.ts`)

**One draft per estimate, saved as the person works.** One working copy, a bar that is always visible with the running totals, the save state, a **Save draft** button (still there, for people who want to press it) and Back / Next.

*When it saves.* About 0.8 seconds after the last keystroke or change (details, notes, items), and at once when the person adds a section, attaches a photo, presses Save draft or presses Finalise / Request approval. Every change to the saved draft goes through ONE queue on the screen (`lib/crm/quotations/save-queue.ts`): saves run one at a time, asks made while a save is waiting are folded into it, and an ask made while a save is running queues exactly one more that includes what was typed meanwhile. Section adds, photo attaches, item deletes, moves and the finish run in order with the saves, so two overlapping saves can never create a second copy of an item or a section. An item keeps its screen key when it gets its saved id (no remount, no lost cursor), and what counts as saved is what was SENT, so an edit typed while a save runs is still saved by the next run.

*What the person sees.* "Changes are saved automatically" (nothing typed yet), "Saving…", "Saved" (only when every write succeeded), "Not saved yet" with the item and the reason (an item that cannot be saved as typed, for example no quantity, stays on screen and is left out of the automatic save until it is complete), and "Could not save" with the reason when a write failed: what was typed stays on screen and is saved again on the next edit or on **Save draft**. Items already written keep their ids, so retrying cannot duplicate them. Leaving with anything unsaved asks first.

### 2.5 Frozen snapshot (the seam for the exports)

`crm_quotation_snapshots.content` (type `QuotationSnapshot`, `packages/domain/src/quotations.ts`): `schema_version` (1), `reference`, `revision`, `issue_date`, `validity_date`, `enquiry`, `issuer`, `bill_to`, `currency`, `vat`, `sections[]`, `totals`, `milestones`, `exclusions`, `terms`. Guard triggers refuse any change, even by a table owner. Read it through `loadQuotation(...).snapshot` (`apps/web/src/lib/crm/quotations/data.ts`).

### 6.1 The defect and its cause

Reproduced on `main` `0dffeba` (browser flow step "a quotation frozen BEFORE this change ... its revision becomes an estimate" failed): after **Create revision** on a quotation issued before the estimates-only change and **Save draft**, the saved revision still held the six clauses "Site access and readiness, Site services and other trades, Changes, Start of work, Scope exclusions, Confidentiality" and the label "Standard terms (working draft, pending legal review)".

Cause, read in code: `crm_quote_create_revision` copies `terms_label` and `terms` from the revised row (`20261220000100_crm_estimates_quotations.sql`, the `insert into crm_quotations` of that function); the app converted a draft only inside `saveEstimateDraft`, `requestQuotationApproval` and `issueQuotation`; and the builder's save sends nothing when nothing was edited (`runSave` in `editor.tsx`: "if (lines.length === 0 && same(details) && same(notes)) return"), so Save draft on an untouched revision never reached the server. What a client could receive was already an estimate without those terms (drafts always export as estimates, and finalising converted first); what was wrong was the saved draft, and that the swap, when it happened, was silent.

### 6.2 What the app does now

* **Nothing is swapped silently.** `saveEstimateDraft` reads the stored terms first; while the draft carries inherited terms it writes them back as they are (with the person's own notes beside them) and does not touch the label. `requestQuotationApproval` and `issueQuotation` refuse such a draft. `issueBlockers` lists the review first, so the Finalise step shows it and Finalise estimate is held.
* **The person sees and confirms.** Section 2.2 rows "Terms of a draft" and "Replace with the current estimate format".
* **The issued document is never touched**: only the draft's own `crm_quotations` row is written; the earlier revision's row, snapshot (guard triggers) and files are as issued (browser flow compares the snapshot hash before and after, and re-exports revision 1).
* **A draft download before the step** is marked DRAFT, uses the estimate layout and leaves the inherited clauses out, as before (`doc-model.ts` builds every draft as an estimate).
* **A revision of a current-format estimate** shows "Selected ... The same format as revision N", needs no step, and saves, finalises and exports as before.
* **The database repeats it (section 6.5, migration `20270111000100_crm_quote_terms_guard.sql`, not applied):** `crm_quote_issue` and `crm_quote_request_approval` refuse a draft that still carries inherited terms, whoever calls them.

### 6.3 Checked against the current estimate workflow

The module reference (section 0 "Format marker", "No acceptance wording"), the browser flow's end state and the code agree on what a client receives from a revision of an earlier quotation: an **Estimate** with the budgetary notice and no standard terms. This change keeps that output exactly and puts a confirmed step in front of it. The flow's expectation that pressing Save draft alone converts the draft was the part that did not match the rule "no silent swap"; the flow now asserts that Save draft keeps the inherited terms and that the confirmed step converts them (`quotations-flow.mjs`, four steps from "its REVISION says the terms were INHERITED"). **Decided by the owner on 2026-11-15** (`../05-data-tests-and-release-ledger.md` section D items 20 to 22): keep the confirmed step; add the database guard (6.5); keep the send history in Estimates with no stage.

### 6.5 The database guard (owner decision, 2026-11-15; migration `20270111000100_crm_quote_terms_guard.sql`, install-order row 39; not applied by Claude)

* **Rule, one definition in two places kept identical:** `termsReview` (`packages/domain/src/estimates.ts`) and `crm_quote_terms_need_review(label, terms)` (the migration). A draft's terms need review unless (1) `terms_label` is an estimate format marker of a known version (`estimate-format:1` or `estimate-format:2`) and (2) every clause of `terms` is titled Assumptions or Next steps. "Confirmed" needs no new column: the confirmed step writes exactly that marker and keeps only those clauses. The same 15 vectors are asserted against both (`packages/domain/test/estimates.test.ts`, `packages/rls-tests/test/crm-quote-terms-guard.rls.test.ts`). A new format version must be added in both places together.
* **What refuses:** `crm_quote_issue` ("Replace the terms carried over into this draft with the current estimate format before finalising it (estimate page, Details step). Nothing was changed") and `crm_quote_request_approval` (the same sentence, "before requesting approval"). Same signatures; each is the applied body of `20261220000100` plus that one check right after the draft is loaded. The app refuses earlier with its own sentence, so staff meet the database's words only through a direct call or a stale page.
* **What it never touches:** issued documents. Both functions load a draft through `crm_quote_load_editable`, which refuses an issued one first; no row, snapshot, file or approval is rewritten or re-validated. Proved: the snapshot and hash of a quotation issued before the migration are identical before and after it, after the refusals and after its revision is finalised; it is still readable and can still be revised (RLS test "ISSUED DOCUMENTS ARE UNTOUCHED"); in the browser it still opens and exports as a Quotation with its terms (`quotations-flow.mjs`).
* **Existing drafts:** not changed. The read-only preflight counts, per company, the drafts that hold inherited terms (and how many are revisions, and how many have an approval waiting) and lists each one. After the migration each needs the confirmed step before it can go out.
* **Rollback:** `crm_quote_terms_guard_90_rollback.sql` restores the exact earlier two functions and drops the rule function.
* **Local fixtures:** a "quotation issued before the change" can no longer be issued on a database that has the guard, so `quotations-flow.mjs` issues its fixture with the guard rolled back and applies it again (as on hosted, where such documents predate it).
