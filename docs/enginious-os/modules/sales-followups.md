## 1. Route and who sees what

`/sales/follow-ups` (`apps/web/src/app/(app)/sales/follow-ups/page.tsx`) is the landing page of Sales: `/sales` redirects here (`sales/page.tsx`), the Sales menu entry, the phone bottom bar "Sales" and the module's own row point here (`nav-groups.ts`, `shell-chrome.tsx`). Any Sales grant reads every open follow-up of its companies (RLS: `crm_has_access(company_id)`, `20261115000100_crm_core_tables.sql`); a person acts on a follow-up only as in section 3.

Data (`loadFollowUps`, `apps/web/src/lib/crm/data.ts`): up to 1000 open `crm_follow_ups` (soonest first) and the 30 most recently completed (`status = done`), with the enquiry (`id, reference, title, stage, company_id, owner_id`, agency, client) or, for a follow-up with no enquiry, the account. Rows whose enquiry or account name cannot be read are dropped (`wrap` returns null). The page also loads the person's unread reminders (`loadMyReminders`, up to 50), all enquiries (`loadOpportunities`) and the handover attention rows (`loadHandoverRows`, `lib/crm/handover-attention-data.ts`). For a handover accepted in the last days, `loadHandoverRows` also reads the person's own "handover accepted" notices (section 2.1).

### 2.1 Dismiss on "Handover accepted" (added 2026-11-12)

"Handover accepted" is news, not an action: the Project Manager accepted the handover and the project exists. It is shown to the Sales owner and the preparer of the handover for 7 days after the acceptance (`ACCEPTED_NOTICE_DAYS`). From `d9e8338` each of them can dismiss their own copy.

**How it works. No new mechanism was added.**

* The acceptance already sends the Sales owner and the preparer one notice each, type `project_handover_accepted`, a row in `notifications` (`proj_accept_handover` calling `proj_notify`, `supabase/migrations/20261120000200_proj_projects_handover_functions.sql`).
* `loadHandoverRows` reads the person's own notices of that type from the last 8 days (the table shows a person only their own rows) and matches them to their handovers by `payload.handover_id`. Unread: the row is shown with **Dismiss**. Read: the row is not shown. An unread notice wins over a read one for the same handover (`lib/crm/handover-attention-data.ts`).
* **Dismiss** calls `dismissHandoverNotice(noticeId)` (`lib/actions/crmHandoverNotice.ts`), which marks that one notice read through the existing `proj_mark_notifications_read` (`supabase/migrations/20261120000400_proj_plans_site_calendar_discussion.sql`). That function changes only rows that belong to the caller, are unread and are Project notices. The action refuses anything that is not one valid id before calling it, because an empty list would mark every Project notice of the person read (test "never calls the database without one valid id").

| Point | Behaviour | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who | The person whose notice it is: the Sales owner or the preparer. Nobody can see or dismiss another person's notice, in the same company or another (the database returns 0 rows changed) | `packages/rls-tests/test/ops-phase2b.rls.test.ts`, test "the Sales owner dismisses their own 'handover accepted' notice..." |
| What changes | `read_at` on that one notice. **Nothing else:** the handover, the project, the other person's notice and the rows that ask for something (Won, handover not submitted; the PM asked a question) are untouched | same test (the handover's status, version and project, and other people's unread notices, compared before and after) |
| Across devices | It is stored on the notice, so the row stays gone on every device and after signing in again | same test |
| Button | **Dismiss** ("Saving…" while it runs). If it could not be saved the row stays and says "Could not dismiss it. Try again." | `components/sales/dismiss-handover-notice.tsx` |
| After | Follow-ups and My Day are refreshed (`revalidatePath("/sales", "layout")`, `revalidatePath("/")`). My Day's **Also open** line "Handover accepted" is built from the same rows (`handoverNotice` in `app/(app)/action-centre.ts`), so a dismissed notice leaves My Day too | `crmHandoverNotice.ts`; `action-centre.ts` |
| Pressed twice, or already dismissed on another device | Not an error: the database changes 0 rows and the refreshed list no longer shows the row | `crmHandoverNotice.test.ts` "already dismissed elsewhere is not an error; a failed save is" |
| Not dismissed | The row goes by itself 7 days after the acceptance, as before | `handover-attention.ts` |

**Consequences to know (all follow from reusing the existing notice):**

1. **Marking all Project notices read in Projects also dismisses it**, because it is the same notice: the **Mark all as read (N)** button on My Work (`app/(app)/projects/work/mark-read-button.tsx`, `markProjectNotificationsRead`) marks every unread Project notice of the person, this one included. It applies to a Sales person who can open My Work; there is no control there that marks one notice alone.
2. **A person who accepted the handover themselves does not see the row.** No notice is sent to the person who acts (`proj_notify` skips the caller), and the row is skipped when `accepted_by` is the viewer. Before this change such a person saw the row for 7 days.
3. **If no notice exists for the person** (for example `proj_notify` sent none because the person's account was inactive at that moment), the row is shown **without Dismiss** and only expires after 7 days.
4. **If the notices cannot be read, the whole Handover card says it could not check** (the message in section 2). A dismissed notice must not reappear, and an undismissed one must not vanish, on a guess.
5. The notice window read is 8 days (`ACCEPTED_NOTICE_DAYS + 1`), a little wider than the 7 days a row is shown, and at most 500 notices.

## 3. Rules and failures of each action

| Action | Starting state | Rules (function) | Notable failures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create (enquiry page: Add follow-up) | Enquiry not Won/Lost ("A closed opportunity has no follow-ups") | `crm_create_follow_up`: any Sales grant for the company; "Assigned to" defaults to the person; for someone other than the person or the owner you must be the owner or hold `assign_owner`; the assignee must hold an active grant for the company | "Only the owner or a founder can assign follow-ups to others"; "The assignee has no Sales access for this company" |
| Complete | Follow-up `open` | Inquiry branch (`20261219000100_crm_clients_agencies.sql`, unchanged text from the original in `20261115000200_crm_workflow_functions.sql`): marks `done` (`completed_at`, `completed_by`, `outcome`); if the enquiry is active and no open follow-up remains: a next follow-up is **assigned to the enquiry owner** (not necessarily the person completing it) or the enquiry is put on hold; otherwise refused; always logs an internal note "Follow-up done: <title>. <outcome>" | "Record the outcome of this follow-up"; "Add a next action or put the opportunity on hold before completing the last follow-up"; "This follow-up is already closed" |
| Reschedule | Follow-up `open` | Updates `due_at`; **deletes the reminders already generated for it** so the new date creates a fresh one (`crm_reminders where follow_up_id`) | "This follow-up is already closed" |
| Cancel | Follow-up `open` | Reason required; refused when it is the only open follow-up of an active enquiry | "Say why this follow-up is being cancelled"; "This is the only next action. Complete it with a new next action, or put the opportunity on hold." |

Won and Lost cancel every open follow-up of the enquiry (`crm_mark_won`, `crm_mark_lost`); reopening requires a new one (`crm_reopen_opportunity`).

## 4. Assignee, account owner, outreach and enquiry owner: four independent things

Verified against the SQL and documented with RLS evidence in `sales-clients-and-agencies.md` section 5: the **follow-up assignee** may complete, reschedule, cancel and reassign that follow-up only (no right on the enquiry or account); the **enquiry owner** edits, moves and closes the enquiry and may assign its follow-ups; the **account owner** is a label with an audit trail and confers no right; **outreach** (pre-enquiry) has its own tables and is not a follow-up. Reassigning a follow-up never changes the owner of the enquiry or account (`crm_reassign_follow_up`), and changing the enquiry owner moves only the previous owner's open follow-ups (`crm_assign_owner`).
