## Add or remove people on a project
- id: projects-people-access
- area: projects
- status: published
- keywords: add someone to a project; remove from project; project members; people and access; department team; department head; temporary leader; acting project manager; change project manager; who is on the project; sales link; project team
- links: Projects -> /projects; My Work -> /projects/work
- screens: /projects/[id]/people

People & access lists everyone on a project, their role and their department team. The Project Manager, a temporary leader or a founder adds and removes people. Only people who already have Projects access can be added. A removed person loses the project's details, files, notices and Chat group straight away. The Project Manager and a department head cannot be removed until someone else takes that role.

1. Open the project, press Manage project and choose People & access.
2. To add someone, choose them under Add a person, pick a department if you want one, and press Add to project.
3. To remove someone, press Remove on their row, choose who takes over their open tasks if asked, and press Remove.
4. For a department, press Add department team, name it and choose its head; use Department on a row to move a person.
5. The Project Manager or a founder can hand over while away: under Temporary project leadership choose a member and a last day, then press Appoint. End now stops it early.

## Client feedback and scope changes
- id: projects-feedback-scope
- area: projects
- status: published
- keywords: client feedback; record feedback; client asked for a change; correction; scope change; propose a scope change; approve scope; reject scope change; decide scope; withdraw proposal; change request; extra work
- links: Projects -> /projects; My Day -> /
- screens: /projects/[id]/changes; /projects/scope-decisions/[scopeChangeId]

Feedback & changes keeps what the client asked for and every change to the agreed scope. The Project Manager records feedback as a correction or as a proposed change. Something outside the agreed scope needs a scope change proposal, and only a person with scope approval can approve or reject it, with a reason. Approving replaces the current scope; the original stays on record. No date or price moves by itself, and a chat message never approves anything.

1. Open the project, press Manage project and choose Feedback & changes.
2. Project Manager: press Record feedback, say who gave it, what was asked and whether it is a correction or a proposed change, then Save feedback.
3. The person responsible presses Update on the entry to set its status and say how it was resolved, declined or deferred.
4. Project Manager: press Propose a scope change, write the whole scope as it would read and the effect on dates, then Record proposal.
5. Scope approver: open the proposal from My Day or on this page, press Decide, choose Approve or Reject, give the reason and press Record decision.

## Complete a project
- id: projects-complete
- area: projects
- status: published
- keywords: complete project; close a project; finish project; completion; client confirmation; completion evidence; unfinished tasks; cancel or defer; completion note; signed handover; equipment still out
- links: Projects -> /projects
- screens: /projects/[id]/complete

Only the Project Manager, a temporary leader or a founder can complete a project. Nothing is marked done for you: every unfinished task, deliverable, milestone, piece of client feedback and upcoming site booking needs your decision and a reason. A client project also needs the client's confirmation. Completing does not return equipment: anything still out stays outstanding until the warehouse records its return. If the page says something could not load, press Retry; what you typed is kept.

1. Open the project, press Manage project and choose Complete project.
2. Under Unfinished work choose Cancel, Defer, Resolve or Decline for each item and write the reason. Apply to all sets a whole group at once.
3. For a client project choose the Kind of confirmation (E-mail reference, Signed handover or Uploaded confirmation), the date the client confirmed and who confirmed.
4. Write the completion note.
5. Press Complete project. The list under Before you can complete the project shows anything still missing.

## Project history and reopening a project
- id: projects-history-reopen
- area: projects
- status: published
- keywords: project history; change log; who changed the date; sales handover snapshot; completion record; reopen project; reopen a completed project; closed too early; what was agreed; history tab
- links: Projects -> /projects
- screens: /projects/[id]/history

History shows three things: the Sales handover as it was accepted, every completion with its evidence and the decisions taken, and the change log of dates, status, the Project Manager and people (the latest 100 changes). It is read-only. A founder, or someone a founder allowed to reopen projects, can reopen a finished project with a reason. The earlier completion stays in History, and anything cancelled or deferred at completion stays that way until the Project Manager reinstates it.

1. Open the project, press Manage project and choose History.
2. Read Sales handover for what was agreed, Completion and reopening for each completion, and Change log for who changed what and why.
3. To reopen a finished project, press Manage project and choose Reopen project. It is offered only to the people who may reopen.
4. Write why it is being reopened and press Reopen. The project returns to In progress.
5. The Project Manager then reinstates the cancelled or deferred items that still matter.
